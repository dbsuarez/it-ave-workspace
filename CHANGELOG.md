# What's new in IT Avenue Workspace

Each version lists what changed, in plain words. The app shows this after it updates.

## 1.2.0 - 2026-11-21
### New
- **Profile card**: click a person's name or picture and a card opens beside it with their photo, full name, job title, status, how many channels you share, their department, location and manager. Type in the box at the bottom and press Enter to message them, use the call button, or open the full profile from the ⋯ menu. Your own picture still opens your menu.
- **Member list**: the Members button in a channel's or forum's header opens a panel on the right with the people in that channel or forum, under Online and Offline (each folds away), with their status lines and a search. Click a person for their card.

- **Cover photo**: on your own page, press **Change cover**, choose a picture and drag it to pick the part to show. It appears at the top of your page and on your profile card. **Remove cover** goes back to your colour. Only you can change your own cover.

### Improved
- **Your own picture in the left rail** now opens the same card as everyone else's, with your photo, full name, job title, status, department, location and manager, above the menu (Set status, Preferences, Sign out...).
- **Direct Messages** now lists only the people you have talked with, the latest conversation first. A new search box at the top finds employees to start a chat with.

## 1.1.1 - 2026-11-20
### Improved
- **Help articles open in a floating window** over the screen you are working in, so you no longer leave it. Drag the window by its title bar, resize it from the corner, and press Esc to close it. "Open in the Knowledge Base" is still there when you want the full page.
- The Help tab for a screen is now a slim tab on the right edge, so it no longer covers buttons such as Share.

### Fixed
- In a call, you now see a camera or screen share that was already on before you joined.
- When screen sharing cannot start, the message now says why.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-11-20
### New
- **Knowledge Base**: answers to common questions, by topic, with search, a find box inside long articles and a floating "On this page" list. The Help button on a screen opens the articles for that screen.
- **Leave**: request leave, see your balance, and follow your request. Managers approve from the Leave tab.
- **Sheets**: spreadsheets you can share, with tables that can feed Leave holidays and credits.
- **Canvas text size**: choose any font size from 8 to 96, and align a table's header and its rows separately.
- **Automatic updates**: the app now tells you when a new version is ready and installs it when you restart.
- **Your own Create menu** (the + in the left rail) with Notes, Drawings, Sheets and more.
- **Customize the rail**: people who manage the workspace can order the left rail and choose what goes under More.

### Improved
- Everyone is shown by full name, with the username only when there is no name.
- Channel permissions can be set for a whole column at a time, and actions you cannot use are switched off instead of failing when you press them.
- Tables in articles keep line breaks and bullet lists inside their cells.

### Fixed
- Bold text around coloured words now shows as bold instead of showing the stars.
- Dark theme: text typed in grids is readable.
