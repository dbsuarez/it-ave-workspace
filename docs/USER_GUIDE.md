# IT Avenue Workspace: User guide

Short answers to the things people ask most. Use the search box at the top of Help to find a word.

## Getting around

### Where is everything?
The strip on the left has the main places: **Home** (channels and forums), **DMs**, **Activity** (mentions, replies and reactions to you), **Later** (saved items and reminders), **Files**, **Notes**, **Canvas**, **People**, **Huddle** (audio calls) and **More** (Lists and Canvases). **Moderation** appears only if you are allowed to moderate or manage the workspace.

### Which channels does everyone have?
The **OFFICIAL** section at the top of the sidebar holds four channels everyone is in: **#welcome**, **#rules** (the workspace rules and code of conduct, so read it first), **#announcements** and **#general**. They are mandatory: everyone is in them, nobody can leave, and they cannot be renamed, archived or deleted. An Owner can make other channels built-in too, and they appear in the same section.

### Keyboard shortcuts
- **Ctrl+K** searches messages, people and files.
- **Ctrl+N** starts a new message.
- **Esc** cancels a reply or closes a menu.

### How do I find something?
Type in the search box at the top. It looks through the messages, people and files you are allowed to see, and nothing else. Typing `/search` followed by words in any channel gives you the same results privately.

## Channels and messages

### How do I send a message, a file or a reply?
Pick a channel on the left, type, and press Enter. The paperclip attaches files (up to 10 at once, or drag them onto the window). Use the reply arrow on a message to answer it, and the smiley to react.

### What is the difference between a channel and a forum?
A channel is a running conversation, shown with a **#**. A forum is a list of separate **topics**, shown with a **speech-bubble icon**. Every department has its own forum (see the next section).

### Slash commands
Type **/** in a channel to see what you can use.
- **/help** lists the commands available to you.
- **/search words** searches, with the answer shown only to you.
- **The poll button** (the little bars icon in the message box) opens a **Create poll** window: type the question, fill in the options (press Enter for the next one, **+ Add option** for more, up to 10), choose **Allow multiple answers**, **Anonymous votes** and when the poll ends, then press **Create poll**. Once a poll has ended, voters show as small round pictures instead of names.
- **To change a poll you made**, open the menu on its message and choose **Edit**: the same window opens with the poll filled in. You can reword the question, rename, add and reorder options, and change when it ends. Once anyone has voted, an option that has votes cannot be removed and "multiple answers" and "anonymous" are locked. Only the person who made a poll can edit it, and only while it is open.
- **/poll "Question?" "Option A" "Option B"** does the same from the keyboard. Add `--multi` to let people pick several, `--anonymous` to hide who voted, and `--closes 2h` to close it automatically.
- **The megaphone button** (in the message box, only in an announcement channel and only if you have the Announce permission) opens a **Post announcement** window: write the announcement, check the **Preview** of how it will look, and press **Post announcement** (or Ctrl+Enter). It is posted straight away, so read the preview first.
- **To change an announcement you posted**, open the menu on its message and choose **Edit**: the same window opens with the text filled in, and **Save changes** updates it. It then shows "(edited)" and the earlier version is kept. You need the Announce permission still, and the limit is the same 2000 characters.
- **/announce text** does the same from the keyboard. It only works in channels marked as announcement channels, and only for people who have the Announce permission. You see a preview first and confirm.

Slash commands that post to everyone (**/poll** and **/announce**) do not work inside a forum, because a forum only takes posts inside topics.

## Department forums

### What is my department's forum?
Each department has a private forum called `dept-` and the department name, for example `dept-mis`. Only people in that department can open it. Find it in the sidebar by its speech-bubble icon. If your administrator has grouped forums into a section, look there.

### How do I start a topic?
Open the forum, press **New topic**, give it a title (up to 120 characters), write the first post, optionally attach files, and press **Start topic**. The topic opens straight away.

### How do I reply?
Click a topic to open it. The posts appear like an ordinary conversation, and you write at the bottom. **← All topics** takes you back to the list.

### What do Active and Archived mean?
**Active** holds the topics in use. A moderator can archive a finished topic, which moves it to **Archived** and makes it read-only. It can be brought back.

### Can I find a topic later?
Yes. Search finds topics by their title, posts by their text, and attachments by their file name (not by what is inside the file).

### Why can't I see a forum, or why did it disappear?
A forum is open only to people currently in that department. If you were moved to another department, you lose that forum straight away and get the new one. An Owner who is not in the department does not see it either, unless the Owner private-channel switch in Moderation is on.

## Moderating a forum

Only the **department head**, an **Owner who can open the forum**, and people whose role has the **Manage messages** permission see these buttons. They are small icons; hover over one to see what it does.

### How do I pin a topic?
Click the **pin** icon on the topic (in the list or at the top of the open topic). Pinned topics move to a **Pinned** group at the top of the forum and stay there, however busy other topics are. A pinned topic works well as that forum's announcement. Click the same icon again to **unpin**.

### How do I lock a topic?
Click the **padlock**. Nobody can reply or edit their own posts in a locked topic, and the message box shows "This topic is locked". Click it again to unlock. A moderator can still remove a post.

### How do I archive, rename or delete a topic?
- **Archive** (box icon) moves it to the Archived tab and makes it read-only; click again to **unarchive**.
- **Rename** (pencil icon) changes the title. The person who started a topic can rename their own while it is open.
- **Delete** (red bin) removes the topic and all its posts for good, after a confirmation. The person who started a topic can delete it only while nobody has replied.

Every pin, lock, archive, rename and delete is recorded in the forum's audit log.

## Notes

Notes are your own private notes, laid out like Google Keep: a wall of cards you can colour, pin, label and search. Open **Notes** in the left rail.

### How do I write a note?
Press **Take a note...** at the top, type a title and the text, and press **Close** (or Esc). There is no Save button: a note saves by itself a moment after you stop typing, and the bottom corner says "Edited at ...". The little tick-list button next to **Take a note...** starts a checklist instead.

### What can I do to a note?
Open a note by clicking it, or hover a card for quick buttons. The row of buttons at the bottom of an open note does these, left to right: **Background options** (twelve colours), **Remind me** (later today, tomorrow morning, next week, or a date and time you pick), **Add image**, **Show checkboxes** (turns the lines into a checklist and back), **Add label**, **Archive**, **Delete**, and undo and redo. The pin at the top right keeps a note in a **Pinned** section at the top of the wall.

### What are labels?
Labels group notes, such as Work or Home. Add them from a note, then pick one in the left menu to see only those notes. **Edit labels** in the menu renames or deletes them; deleting a label keeps the notes.

### How do I find a note?
Type in **Search notes**: it looks in titles, text, checklist items and label names. **Reminders** in the left menu shows the notes that have a reminder set. When a reminder is due you get the usual reminder pop-up, and it also appears in **Later**.

### What do Archive and Trash do?
**Archive** tucks a note away without deleting it; find it under **Archive**, and unarchive or pin it to bring it back. **Delete** moves a note to **Trash**, where it stays 30 days and can be restored; after that, or when you press **Empty Trash** or **Delete forever**, it is gone. A note you empty out completely is thrown away when you close it.

### Limits
You can keep 1000 notes, 50 labels, 200 items in a checklist and 10 pictures (PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, up to 2 MB each) on a note. Notes are private to you and cannot be shared.

## The left rail

The rail on the left lists the sections of the app. If your window is short, scroll the rail with the mouse wheel (there is no scrollbar): the **More** button, the **+** (Create) button and your picture always stay in view at the bottom.

### Can the rail be rearranged?
Yes, by an Owner (anyone who manages the workspace), and it applies to **everyone**. Open your picture menu and choose **Customize the rail**: move sections up and down, or send a section into the **More** menu (and bring it back). Home always stays first. **Save for everyone** applies it at once for everybody who is signed in; **Reset to default** puts it back as it was. Employees cannot change it. A section such as Moderation still only shows for the people who may use it.

## Canvas text: size, colour and table alignment

### Can I choose the font size?
Yes. Select the text and open **Size** in the Canvas toolbar: pick one of the ready sizes (10 to 64) or type your own between 8 and 96 pixels and press **Apply**. **Default** brings the text back to normal. Size works together with colour, bold and italic, and it is kept when you export to PDF or Word. Headings (H1 to H3) still work as before.

### Can the header and the rows of a table be aligned differently?
Yes. Click in a cell, open **Align** in the table bar and choose what to align: **Whole column** (header and rows together, as before), **Header cell** (only this column's header) or **Rows below** (only the cells under the header). So you can centre the headers and keep the numbers on the right.

## Knowledge Base

The Knowledge Base is where HR keeps the answers to common questions and the employee handbook. Open **Knowledge Base** in the left rail.

### How do I find an answer?
Type in the search box at the top ("leave", "payslip", "dress code"): results appear as you type, with the category, the date it was last updated and a short piece of the text. Or open a category and read down its list of articles, in order like a table of contents. Each article shows **Updated <date> by <name>** so you know how fresh it is, and **Previous** and **Next** buttons to read the chapters in order.

### Can I search inside a long article?
Yes. Every article has a **Find in this article** box under its title (or press **Ctrl+F** while the article is open). Matches are highlighted and the box says "2 of 14"; press **Enter** (or the arrows) for the next one, **Shift+Enter** for the previous one, and **Esc** to clear. An article with two or more headings also shows a floating **On this page** list at the top right: click a heading to jump to it, and click the list's title to fold it away (the app remembers your choice).

### How do I back up the Knowledge Base, and restore it?
On the Knowledge Base home screen (people who manage it only) press **Backup**. Tick the categories and articles to save, and tick what else to include (Pictures and Help links are on by default; Version history and Comments are optional), then **Download backup**. You get one file to keep somewhere safe. To bring things back press **Restore** and choose that file: it lists what is inside, you tick the articles to restore, and for an article that already exists you choose to **skip** it, **replace the text** (the old text stays in History) or **add a copy**. Nothing is changed unless you choose it.

### Does a Workspace Reset delete the Knowledge Base?
Not by default. The reset panel has **Keep the Knowledge Base** ticked: the articles, pictures, comments and history are packed before the reset and put back after it. Untick it to wipe the Knowledge Base too. A copy of what was kept is also saved in the backups folder, and you can restore from it with **Restore**.

### Can I save it as a PDF?
In the desktop app, **Save as PDF** on an article saves that article; on a category it saves all of its published articles as one document, for example the whole handbook.

### Who can write in it?
People with the **Manage knowledge base** permission (Owners, and the HR role by default). Everyone else can read and search only.

### How does HR add content?
- **New category** on the Knowledge Base screen (for example Leave, Payroll, Employee Handbook). **Rename**, **Delete** (this deletes its articles too) and the up and down arrows (put the articles in reading order) are on the category page.
- **New article**: give it a title, a category and (optionally) the screens where it should appear as a **Help** link, then write the text in the Canvas editor that opens: headings, lists, tables, pictures (the **Image** button), callouts, **Comments** and **History** are all there, and **Export** saves it as PDF or Word. **Back to the Knowledge Base** takes you back to the article. On an article, **Edit text** reopens the editor and **Details** changes the title, category, published state and Help screens. An article starts as a **Draft** that only HR can see; tick **Published** when it is ready. Employees never see the editor, and these articles do not appear in anyone's own Canvas list.
- **Help links:** in **Details**, tick the screens an article helps with (for example Leave for "How do I file a leave?"). People on that screen then see a **Help** button at the top right that opens the article.
- **Import**: paste a long text (for example the handbook after another tool turned your screenshots into text) and choose whether each `#` or each `##` heading starts a new article. You see the list of articles before anything is saved. They are added at the end of the category as drafts (or published, if you tick that), so you can check each one and fix it. Text before the first heading becomes an article called Introduction.

## Sheets

Sheets are spreadsheets you make inside the app. They look and work like a normal spreadsheet (type, paste from Excel, formulas, several tabs), they save by themselves, and the rest of the app can read the data you publish from them. Open **Sheets** in the left rail.

### Who can make a sheet?
People with the **Manage sheets** permission (Owners, and the HR role by default). Everyone else sees only the sheets shared with them, under **Shared with me**.

### How do I work in a sheet?
Press **New sheet**, name it, and type. There is no Save button: the bar says "Unsaved changes", then "Saved" a moment later. The **+** beside the tabs adds a tab; right-click a cell for rows and columns. If someone else saved the sheet while you were editing, your changes are merged into theirs and a note tells you (when you both changed the same cell, yours is kept). Adding or deleting rows while someone else edits the same sheet can mix cells up, so take turns for that.

### How do I share a sheet?
The owner presses **Share**, picks a person and chooses **Can edit** or **Can view**. Only members can be added. Only the owner can share or delete the sheet. Being an Owner of the workspace does not open other people's sheets.

### How do I get data in and out?
**Import CSV** adds the rows of a CSV file (from Excel, with commas, semicolons or tabs) as a new tab. **Export CSV** saves the open tab as a CSV that Excel opens; cells that could run as a formula in Excel are marked with a leading apostrophe. Pasting straight from Excel into the grid also works.

### What is a table, and what is Publish?
The rest of the app should never read a sheet someone is half-way through typing. So a tab can be declared a **table**: press **Tables**, **New table**, choose the tab, name the table (lowercase letters, digits and underscores, for example `holidays`), and give each column a name and a type: text, number, date (YYYY-MM-DD), yes/no or person (a username). **Use the header row of the tab** fills the column names for you; the first row of the tab must match them. **Check** lists every problem by row and column ("Row 3, Date: 2027-02-30 is not a real date"). **Publish** saves the rows only when there are none, and what was published is what the rest of the app reads. Keep editing as much as you like: nothing changes for the app until you press **Publish** again, and the list tells you when the sheet has changed since. Formulas are published as their results.

### Can I start from the leave records?
HR can press **New from leave records** on the Sheets screen: it makes a sheet with a copy of the current and upcoming leave (pending and approved), one row per request. It is a copy to work with, not a live link.

### Can a table fill in Leave?
Yes, for two things: the **company holidays** and each person's **leave credits**. In the Tables dialog (people with Manage sheets), choose **Use this table in Leave**. It fills in the columns Leave needs: `date` and `name` for holidays; `person` (a username), `leave_type` (for example Vacation Leave), `year` and `days` for credits (extra days on top of the type's allowance, in halves, negative to remove). Only one table can feed holidays and one can feed credits at a time; choosing another asks before switching.

Nothing changes in Leave while you type. When you press **Publish**, a preview shows exactly what would change in Leave: what is added, renamed or removed (holidays), and what is set or goes back to the default (credits). Press **Apply and publish** to do it, or **Cancel** to change nothing. The sheet is the whole list for the years it contains: a holiday or credit in one of those years that the sheet does not list is removed (the preview shows these in red). Years the sheet does not mention are left alone. Leave's own holiday editor and the leave adjustment boxes become read-only for those years and say which sheet manages them. Days already counted on existing leave requests do not change.

If you stop using a table for Leave (or delete the sheet), Leave keeps what was applied and can be edited by hand again.

### Can I start next year's holidays from this year's?
Press **Holidays from last year** on the Sheets screen and type the year. It makes a sheet named "Holidays <year>" with the previous year's company holidays moved to that year (Feb 29 becomes Feb 28) and connects it to Leave when no other sheet does. Correct the moving holidays by hand (Holy Week, Eid and the days announced each year), then Publish and check the preview.

## Drawings

Drawings are whiteboards for quick sketches, diagrams and documenting how a system or process works. They are built on Excalidraw and work without any internet access beyond your workspace.

### How do I start a drawing?
Three ways. Open **Drawings** in the left rail and press **New drawing** for a drawing of your own. In any channel, open its **Drawings** tab and press **New drawing**. Or, while writing in a channel or a forum topic, press the pencil button beside the paperclip: the drawing is added to the conversation as a card anyone there can open.

A drawing added to a conversation shows as a picture of the drawing, with its name underneath; click the picture to open it. The picture is saved by whoever last edited the drawing, a few seconds after they stop, so it follows the drawing. A drawing nobody has opened since this was added shows a plain tile ("No preview yet") until someone who can edit it opens it.

### Who can see and change a drawing?
A drawing in a channel or topic follows that conversation: whoever can open the conversation can open the drawing, and whoever can post there can draw. Guests, and conversations that are archived or locked, are view-only. A drawing of your own is private until you share it: press **Share** in the drawing, add people by name, and choose **Editor** or **Viewer** for each. **Share as a copy** gives someone their own copy instead.

### Can we draw together?
Yes. Everyone who has the drawing open sees each other's shapes appear as they are drawn, and sees each other's pointers and names. The top bar shows who is here and whether your work is **Saved**.

### Can I draw without a connection?
Yes. Your drawing is kept on this computer as you work, and the bar says **Offline: will sync**. When the connection returns, your changes are merged with what others did meanwhile and the bar goes back to **Saved**. If you and someone else changed the very same shape while apart, the later change wins; everything else from both of you is kept. A drawing started offline is created on the server the first time you are connected.

### How do I keep a copy of a drawing?
Press **Export PNG** or **Export SVG** in the top bar for a picture. To keep the drawing itself, press **Save file**: you get a `.excalidraw` file that opens in any Excalidraw, including excalidraw.com.

### How do I open a .excalidraw file?
On the Drawings screen press **Open file** to make a new drawing of your own from a file. Inside a drawing you can edit, **Open file** instead adds the file's shapes to that drawing; nothing already there is removed, so it is safe while others are drawing. Files from excalidraw.com work too.

### Can I make a diagram from text?
Yes. Open the shapes button at the end of the toolbar and choose **Mermaid to Excalidraw**. Type a Mermaid description on the left and the diagram appears on the right; press **Insert** to put it in the drawing as shapes you can edit. Flowcharts, sequence diagrams and class diagrams become shapes. Not sure how to start? Above the text box, **Start from a template** offers ready-made examples of each kind (steps in order, a decision, a network layout, a request and reply, a sign-in, people and roles, equipment and owners). Pick one and change the words.

### Why can't I change a drawing, or why does it say "Cannot sync"?
View-only drawings show a note saying why (a Viewer share, a guest account, or an archived or locked conversation). **Cannot sync** means you lost access or the drawing was deleted; your copy stays on this computer, so export it to keep it.

### Who can delete a drawing?
The person who made it, and moderators of the conversation it belongs to. Deleting a drawing in a conversation turns its card into "Drawing removed".

## People

### Where do I see who works here?
**People** has the **Directory** (switch between cards and a list, and show active or inactive people), **My team** (your direct reports), the **Org chart**, **Leave**, and **Attendance**. **Manage** appears for HR and Owners.

### How do I save the org chart as a picture?
Open **People > Org chart** and press **SVG** or **PNG** under **Save as** in the chart's toolbar. The picture shows the chart as it is on screen: the search filter is applied, and a team you have collapsed stays collapsed and shows "+N hidden" (expand everyone first to save the whole company). It includes photos, titles, departments and the dotted "also reports to" lines, with the date underneath. **SVG** is the best choice: it stays sharp at any size, even on a poster, and opens in a browser, Word, PowerPoint or Inkscape. **PNG** is for places that only take pictures; it is saved twice as sharp as the screen, and PNG keeps text and lines crisp, which JPG would blur.

### Who is the head of a department?
Open **People > Manage > Departments**: each department shows its head. Ask HR or an Owner to change it.

## For administrators

### How do I set a department head, or rename a department?
Go to **People > Manage > Departments** (HR and Owners).
1. Change the name, or click the head box and start typing a name. The list is alphabetical and shows `Name (username)`. Clear the box for no head.
2. Rows you changed get a purple bar, and the footer counts the unsaved changes.
3. Press **Save changes** once. Only the changed departments are saved.

The new head becomes the moderator of that department's forum immediately, and the old head loses it. Moderators someone added by hand keep their role.

### How do I add a department?
In the same dialog, fill in **New department** and press **Add**. Its forum is created automatically. Deleting a department archives its forum and keeps its content.

### How do I group channels and forums into a section?
Open the **Channel organizer**: click the gear icon beside **Channels** in the sidebar.
1. Under **Workspace sections**, press **+ Add** and name the section.
2. Move channels and forums into it with the dropdown beside each one, or drag them in.
3. Press **Save sections**.

Forums appear in the list for administrators by name only: you can place them in a section without being able to read them. Members only see a forum in the section if they are allowed to open it. Forums are created by creating a department, not by hand.

### How do I make a channel built-in, or change the order?
Only an **Owner** can do this.
- **Make one built-in:** open the channel's settings (**Moderation** > the channel), find **Built-in channel**, and press **Make built-in**. Everyone is added to it at once, new people join automatically, nobody can leave or be removed, and it cannot be renamed, archived or deleted. It moves into the **OFFICIAL** section. Press **Turn off built-in** to make it an ordinary channel again; people stay in it, but it leaves OFFICIAL. You can also tick **Built-in channel** when creating a channel.
- **What can be built-in:** a public, shared channel. Personal channels, private channels and department forums cannot. #welcome, #rules, #announcements and #general are required by the app and always stay built-in.
- **Change the order:** open the **Channel organizer**, and use the up and down arrows next to each channel in the **OFFICIAL** section. The new order is saved straight away and everyone sees it.

### How do I give someone more rights?
**Moderation > Roles** holds the roles and the **Assignments** table (the header stays visible as you scroll). Give a role the **Manage messages** permission to let its members moderate every forum and channel they can open. Roles also decide the colour of a person's name.

### How do I give a role to everyone in a department automatically?
In **Moderation > Roles**, press **Edit** on a role and tick the departments under **Given automatically to members of**. Everyone in those departments gets the role now, anyone who joins later gets it too, and anyone who leaves loses it. In the Assignments table such a box is ticked and cannot be changed by hand (hover it to see why); to take the role away from one person, remove them from the department or change the role's departments. A role someone was given by hand is never taken away by this, and the built-in Owner and Everyone roles can never be given this way.

Good to know: a person belongs to every department listed on their record, not only their main one. Changing someone's **main** department on their record keeps the old one as an extra, so they keep what it gave. To take them out of the old department, remove it in the department list on their record. You can only choose roles below your own rank, and every change appears in the audit log.

### Why can an Owner not read a private channel or forum?
By design. Owners see private channels and forums they are not members of only when the **private-channel switch** (Moderation, Superadmin tab) is on, and turning it on is recorded.

### The sidebar does not look right, or something is missing
Check you are a member of the department or channel, then reload with **Ctrl+R**. If it is still wrong, tell an administrator what you expected to see and in which section.

### How do I keep the list of workspace backups tidy?
Under **Moderation > Recovery**, every backup has icon buttons (hover one to see its full name): **rename** (give it a name such as "Before payroll change" so you can tell it apart; the date stays beside it), **keep** (the star: a backup marked Keep is never deleted by a clean-up and cannot be deleted until Keep is turned off), **download** the database or the uploaded files, **check** that it can be read, **restore** from it, and **delete** it. Backups made by the app say why, for example "Before a workspace reset".

A backup that stopped halfway (missing its database or its uploaded files) cannot be restored. These are hidden behind a line such as "2 incomplete backups are hidden": the eye button shows them and the bin button deletes them all. The broom button above the list deletes every complete backup except the newest ones you choose to keep (and those marked Keep).
